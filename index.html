Why I do this

From no network to leading digital literacy

  1. Childhood

    One bar of signal

    I grew up in Meghamalai, a hill station in Tamil Nadu that still has no 3G. To make a call, I walked to a tree or climbed higher to catch a single bar.

  2. First year, UG

    Scared to touch a computer

    I had never used one. Then the Tamil Nadu Government's free laptop scheme gave me my first laptop. One device, one chance, and a new world opened.

  3. MSW

    Go and create it

    Social work taught me that development is something you build in a community, not something you read about.

  4. Nov 2025

    The same fear, in other children

    In an outdoor slum in Uttar Pradesh that Google Maps doesn't even name, I saw children who had never touched a tablet. We taught tablet use, computer basics, digital safety and an introduction to AI, and gave 10 tablets as the first step.

If you want to see real development, go and create it. One community is enough to change your soul.What my social work professors at Madurai Institute of Social Sciences always said
The mission

10→1,000

The first 10 tablets were given through Thaagam Foundation. Ten to a thousand, one slum to many. Coming from a place without network to building digital access for children who are not even on the map feels like life coming full circle.
