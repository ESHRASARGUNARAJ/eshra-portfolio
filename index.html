Case studies

Two programs, start to finish

The problem, what I did, and what changed.

Thaagam Foundation · Noida & Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

356 migrant children into government schools

Problem
Children of migrant families were out of school. My survey of 500+ households in 13 slum clusters found 62% of children aged 6–14 out of school, with 62% of families blocked by missing transfer certificates or documents. Yet 98% of the children said they wanted to study.
What I did
Mobilised families community by community, prepared and supported the documentation, and worked with district education authorities to admit children under the Right to Education Act.
Result
356 children enrolled in government schools, and 1,000+ children reached through AI foundation classes across 12 slum communities with a team of about 25 teachers.

StratSchool with EDII-TN & StartupTN · Tamil Nadu

500+ founders from fear of AI to using it

Problem
Founders and small-business owners knew AI mattered but felt it wasn't for them, so they froze instead of trying it.
What I did
Plain-language, hands-on sessions with reflection built in. Built the tools learners keep using: a Global AI Tools Directory (209 tools, 60 categories), a CRM training deck, a 3-day YouTube for Entrepreneurs workshop and an AI guide for women's Self-Help Groups.
Result
500+ founders trained across 20+ workshops of 50 to 100 people, with participants praising the delivery style and how complex ideas were made simple.

Why I do this

From no network to leading digital literacy

  1. Childhood

    One bar of signal

    I grew up in Meghamalai, a hill station in Tamil Nadu that still has no 3G. To make a call, I walked to a tree or climbed higher to catch a single bar.

  2. First year, UG

    Scared to touch a computer

    I had never used one. Then the Tamil Nadu Government's free laptop scheme gave me my first laptop. One device, one chance, and a new world opened.

  3. MSW

    Go and create it

    Social work taught me that development is something you build in a community, not something you read about.

  4. Nov 2025

    The same fear, in other children

    In an outdoor slum in Uttar Pradesh that Google Maps doesn't even name, I saw children who had never touched a tablet. We taught tablet use, computer basics, digital safety and an introduction to AI, and gave 10 tablets as the first step.

If you want to see real development, go and create it. One community is enough to change your soul.What my social work professors at Madurai Institute of Social Sciences always said
The mission

10→1,000

The first 10 tablets were given through Thaagam Foundation. Ten to a thousand, one slum to many. Coming from a place without network to building digital access for children who are not even on the map feels like life coming full circle.
